> Use this for a change large enough that several people need to agree on
> *how* before work starts. Smaller decisions belong in an ADR; requirements
> belong in a spec.

## 1. Summary
Three sentences. What we are building and the shape of the approach.

## 2. Goals / Non-goals
Explicit both ways. Non-goals prevent the review from expanding indefinitely.

## 3. Current state
How it works today, honestly, including the parts that are bad.

## 4. Proposed design
The structure: components, responsibilities, data flow, boundaries. A context
and container diagram (text-based, checked in). Enough detail that a reader
could find the first bug in it.

## 5. Interfaces and data
APIs, events, schema changes. Compatibility stance for each.

## 6. Failure behavior
Per dependency and per component: failure modes, blast radius, degradation.

## 7. Operational impact
Deployment, migration, capacity, cost, on-call burden, new alerts and runbooks.

## 8. Security and privacy
Trust boundaries, authorization model, personal data, threat-model summary.

## 9. Testing strategy
What is verified at which level; what the acceptance evidence will be.

## 10. Rollout and rollback
Phases, flags, observation windows, the undo for each phase.

## 11. Alternatives
Each one, what it was better at, why it was not chosen.

## 12. Open questions
Question · who decides · by when.
